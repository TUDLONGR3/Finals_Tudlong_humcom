About NOVA

Our Story

Founded in 2014, NOVA began with a simple vision to create a modeling agency that values authenticity, elegance, and professionalism, It is also a chance for people with disabilities Based in Baguio City, we have grown from a small creative studio into a premier agency representing talented individuals across the country.

We believe that true beauty is timeless. Our approach combines classic elegance with modern vision helping our models build sustainable careers while staying true to themselves.

Every face at NOVA tells a unique story. We are here to help you tell yours.
